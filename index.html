I miss you more than I know how to explain, and sometimes the feeling gets tangled when I try to say it out loud.

So I made this little corner of the internet to say it gently: I care about you deeply. I admire the person you are, and I’m grateful for every memory that has you in it.

01

I hope that, in another life, I’ll be enough for you. In this life, I hope you might choose me—if your heart genuinely feels the same. And if it did, I’d hope we could make a whole life together, one ordinary, beautiful day at a time.

And if there’s still a little room for us in your heart, would you be open to one honest conversation about trying again? Only if you want to.

That is a hope, not a promise you owe me. Your feelings are yours, and I’ll respect them. I just wanted you to know mine.
